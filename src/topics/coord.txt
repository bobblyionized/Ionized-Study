=== LESSONS ===
<script type="text/plain" id="L-coord-line">
<h2>Lines &amp; Slopes</h2>
<div class="box"><h4>Formulas</h4><ul>
<li>Slope $m=\frac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-x_1}$. Point-slope form: $y-y_1=m(x-x_1)$.</li>
<li>Parallel lines have equal slopes; perpendicular slopes multiply to $-1$.</li>
<li>Intercept form $\frac xa+\frac yb=1$ crosses the axes at $(a,0)$ and $(0,b)$. With the axes it makes a right triangle of area $\frac{|ab|}{2}$.</li>
<li>Three points are collinear exactly when the slopes between pairs are equal.</li>
<li>For $ax+by=c$ the slope is $-\frac ab$, the $x$-intercept $\frac ca$, the $y$-intercept $\frac cb$.</li>
</ul></div>
<p>To intersect two lines, set the expressions for $y$ equal. To find a point on a line with some property (e.g. $x=y$), substitute the property into the line's equation.</p>
</script>
<script type="text/plain" id="L-coord-dist">
<h2>Distance, Midpoints &amp; Circles</h2>
<div class="box"><h4>Formulas</h4><ul>
<li>Distance: $\sqrt{(x_2-x_1)^2+(y_2-y_1)^2}$. Midpoint: $\left(\frac{x_1+x_2}2,\frac{y_1+y_2}2\right)$.</li>
<li>Circle: $(x-h)^2+(y-k)^2=r^2$. From $x^2+y^2+Dx+Ey+F=0$, complete the square: center $\left(-\frac D2,-\frac E2\right)$, $r^2=\frac{D^2+E^2}{4}-F$.</li>
<li>Point to line $ax+by+c=0$: $\dfrac{|ax_0+by_0+c|}{\sqrt{a^2+b^2}}$.</li>
<li>Parallel lines $ax+by=c_1$ and $ax+by=c_2$ are $\dfrac{|c_1-c_2|}{\sqrt{a^2+b^2}}$ apart.</li>
<li>A line at distance $d$ from a circle's center cuts a chord of length $2\sqrt{r^2-d^2}$.</li>
</ul></div>
<p>"Equidistant from $A$ and $B$" means the point lies on the perpendicular bisector of $AB$. Set the squared distances equal; the squared terms cancel.</p>
</script>
<script type="text/plain" id="L-coord-lattice">
<h2>Lattice Points</h2>
<div class="box"><h4>Counting tools</h4><ul>
<li>On a segment: $\gcd(|\Delta x|,|\Delta y|)+1$ points, including the endpoints.</li>
<li>On $xy=n$: positive solutions $=d(n)$ (the number of divisors); all integer solutions $=2d(n)$.</li>
<li>On $ax+by=c$: find one solution, then step by $\left(\frac{b}{g},-\frac{a}{g}\right)$, and count the steps that stay in range.</li>
<li>On a circle $x^2+y^2=r^2$: list the representations as a sum of two squares, counting signs and order. For example $25=0+25=9+16$ gives $12$ points.</li>
<li>In a region: count column by column (fix $x$, count valid $y$).</li>
<li>$x,y\ge0$ with $x+y\le n$: $\binom{n+2}{2}$ points.</li>
</ul></div>
</script>

<h3>Shortest path with a reflection</h3>
<p>To go from $A$ to a line and then to $B$ (with $A$ and $B$ on the same side), reflect $B$ across the line. The shortest path length is the straight distance from $A$ to the reflected point.</p>
<h3>Regions</h3>
<p>Sketch each inequality's boundary, then compute the area using triangles, rectangles, and circular sectors.</p>
