<script type="text/plain" id="L-count-basic">
<h2>Multiplication Principle &amp; Permutations</h2>
<div class="box"><h4>Core rules</h4><ul>
<li><b>Multiplication principle:</b> for independent stages with $m$ and $n$ options, there are $mn$ outcomes.</li>
<li>Ordered selections of $k$ from $n$ (no repeats): $P(n,k)=\dfrac{n!}{(n-k)!}$.</li>
<li>Arrangements of a word with repeated letters: $\dfrac{n!}{a!\,b!\,\cdots}$ (e.g. BANANA: $\frac{6!}{3!2!}=60$).</li>
<li>With repetition allowed, each of the $k$ slots has $n$ choices: $n^k$.</li>
</ul></div>
<h3>Handle restrictions first</h3>
<p>Fill the most restricted position first. For even 3-digit numbers with distinct digits, split by whether the last digit is $0$ ($9\cdot8$) or not ($4\cdot8\cdot8$): $72+256=328$.</p>
<h3>Counting by complement</h3>
<p>Numbers below 1000 with no digit 7: treat them as 3-digit strings with leading zeros, giving $9^3=729$ strings, then remove $000$: $728$.</p>
</script>
<script type="text/plain" id="L-count-comb">
<h2>Combinations</h2>
<div class="box"><h4>$\binom nk=\dfrac{n!}{k!(n-k)!}$: unordered choices</h4><ul>
<li>Committees: multiply the independent choices, e.g. $\binom52\binom63$ for 2 men and 3 women.</li>
<li>Lattice paths from $(0,0)$ to $(m,n)$ using right/up steps: $\binom{m+n}{m}$.</li>
<li>Handshakes, or segments between $n$ points: $\binom n2$. Triangles from $n$ points with no three collinear: $\binom n3$.</li>
<li>Rectangles in an $m\times n$ grid of squares: $\binom{m+1}{2}\binom{n+1}{2}$ (choose 2 vertical and 2 horizontal lines).</li>
</ul></div>
<h3>"At least" means use the complement</h3>
<p>At least one girl $=$ all committees $-$ committees with no girls.</p>
<h3>Paths avoiding a point</h3>
<p>Total paths $-$ (paths to the point)·(paths from the point).</p>
</script>
<script type="text/plain" id="L-count-stars">
<h2>Stars and Bars</h2>
<div class="box"><h4>Distributing $n$ identical items into $k$ distinct boxes</h4><ul>
<li>Empty boxes allowed (nonnegative solutions of $x_1+\dots+x_k=n$): $\dbinom{n+k-1}{k-1}$.</li>
<li>Every box nonempty (positive solutions): $\dbinom{n-1}{k-1}$.</li>
<li>At least $m$ per box: give $m$ to each first, then distribute the remaining $n-km$ freely.</li>
<li>Upper bounds (each $\le c$): subtract the cases where some $x_i\ge c+1$ (inclusion–exclusion).</li>
</ul></div>
<h3>Recognize it</h3>
<ul><li>Choosing $n$ items from $k$ types with repetition (donuts): $\binom{n+k-1}{k-1}$.</li>
<li>The number of terms in $(a+b+c)^n$ is $\binom{n+2}{2}$.</li>
<li>Digit sums: digits are boxes with a cap of 9, and the leading digit must be at least 1.</li></ul>
</script>
<script type="text/plain" id="L-count-pie">
<h2>Inclusion–Exclusion &amp; Complements</h2>
<div class="box"><h4>Formulas</h4><ul>
<li>$|A\cup B|=|A|+|B|-|A\cap B|$.</li>
<li>$|A\cup B\cup C|=\sum|A|-\sum|A\cap B|+|A\cap B\cap C|$.</li>
<li>Complement: "at least one" $=$ total $-$ "none".</li>
<li>Derangements (nobody in their own spot): $D_n=n!\left(1-\frac1{1!}+\frac1{2!}-\cdots\right)$: $D_2=1, D_3=2, D_4=9, D_5=44, D_6=265$.</li>
</ul></div>
<h3>Divisibility counting</h3>
<p>Integers up to $N$ divisible by none of $2,3,5$: $N-\left(\lfloor\frac N2\rfloor+\lfloor\frac N3\rfloor+\lfloor\frac N5\rfloor\right)+\left(\lfloor\frac N6\rfloor+\lfloor\frac N{10}\rfloor+\lfloor\frac N{15}\rfloor\right)-\lfloor\frac N{30}\rfloor$.</p>
<h3>Squares or cubes</h3>
<p>Count the squares and the cubes, then subtract the sixth powers, which were counted twice.</p>
</script>
<script type="text/plain" id="L-count-arr">
<h2>Arrangements with Restrictions</h2>
<div class="box"><h4>Techniques</h4><ul>
<li><b>Together:</b> glue the group into one block, arrange the blocks, then arrange inside the block.</li>
<li><b>Apart:</b> total $-$ together, or the <b>gap method</b>: arrange the others, then place the restricted items in the gaps.</li>
<li><b>Circular:</b> $n$ people around a table give $(n-1)!$ arrangements (rotations are the same). If reflections also count as the same (a bracelet): $\frac{(n-1)!}{2}$.</li>
<li><b>A before B</b> (not necessarily adjacent): by symmetry, exactly half of all arrangements.</li>
<li><b>Forbidden positions:</b> use inclusion–exclusion on the bad events.</li>
</ul></div>
<div class="ex"><b class="lbl">Example.</b> 4 boys and 3 girls stand in a row with no two girls adjacent.
<details><summary>Solution</summary>Arrange the boys ($4!=24$); they create 5 gaps. Place the girls in 3 different gaps in order: $5\cdot4\cdot3=60$. Total $1440$.</details></div>
</script>
