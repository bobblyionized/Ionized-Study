<h2>4.6.A Absolute Value Inequalities</h2>
<p>$|a-b|$ is the <b>distance</b> between $a$ and $b$ on the number line. Read this way, absolute value inequalities become pictures: $|x-5|\lt2$ says "$x$ is within $2$ of $5$," so $3\lt x\lt7$.</p>

<h3>1. The two basic forms</h3>
<div class="box">For $r\gt0$:
<ul><li>$|x-c|\lt r\iff c-r\lt x\lt c+r$ (within $r$ of $c$: one interval)</li>
<li>$|x-c|\gt r\iff x\lt c-r\ \text{ or }\ x\gt c+r$ (farther than $r$ from $c$: two rays)</li></ul>
The same holds with $\le$ and $\ge$, including the endpoints.</div>
<p>In general, $|u|\lt r$ means $-r\lt u\lt r$, and $|u|\gt r$ means $u\lt-r$ or $u\gt r$, where $u$ can be any expression.</p>

<h3>2. Linear expressions inside</h3>
<p>$|3x-2|\le7$ becomes $-7\le3x-2\le7$, so $-5\le3x\le9$ and $-\frac53\le x\le3$.</p>
<p>$|2x+1|\gt5$ becomes $2x+1\lt-5$ <b>or</b> $2x+1\gt5$: $x\lt-3$ or $x\gt2$, i.e. $(-\infty,-3)\cup(2,\infty)$.</p>
<p>To read off the center and radius: $|ax+b|\lt r$ is $\left|x+\frac ba\right|\lt\frac r{|a|}$, an interval centered at $-\frac ba$ with half-width $\frac r{|a|}$.</p>

<h3>3. Special cases</h3>
<ul><li>$|u|\lt r$ with $r\le0$: <b>no solutions</b> (absolute values are never negative). $|u|\le0$ only when $u=0$.</li>
<li>$|u|\gt r$ with $r\lt0$: <b>every</b> $x$ in the domain works.</li>
<li>$|u|\ge0$: always true.</li></ul>

<h3>4. Nonlinear expressions</h3>
<p>$|x^2-4|\lt3$ means $-3\lt x^2-4\lt3$, i.e. $1\lt x^2\lt7$. That's $1\lt|x|\lt\sqrt7$: $(-\sqrt7,-1)\cup(1,\sqrt7)$.</p>
<p>When the right side also contains $x$, as in $|x-1|\lt2x$, split into cases ($x\ge1$ and $x\lt1$) or solve graphically by finding where the graphs cross.</p>
<div class="lfigs"><div class="lplot" data-f="abs(x-5);2" data-x="0,9" data-y="-1,5" data-w="230" data-cap="$|x-5|\lt2$: the V is below the line $y=2$ for $3\lt x\lt7$"></div></div>

<h3>5. Applications: tolerance</h3>
<p>"A part must be $4.2$ cm long, with a tolerance of $0.05$ cm" means $|L-4.2|\le0.05$: $4.15\le L\le4.25$. Conversely, the interval $[a,b]$ is $\left|x-\frac{a+b}2\right|\le\frac{b-a}{2}$: center the midpoint, radius half the length.</p>

<h3>Worked examples</h3>
<div class="ex"><b class="lbl">Example 1.</b> Write $[-1,9]$ as an absolute value inequality.
<details><summary>Solution</summary>Midpoint $4$, half-length $5$: $|x-4|\le5$.</details></div>
<div class="ex"><b class="lbl">Example 2.</b> How many integers satisfy $|2x-5|\lt7$?
<details><summary>Solution</summary>$-7\lt2x-5\lt7\Rightarrow-2\lt2x\lt12\Rightarrow-1\lt x\lt6$: the integers $0,1,2,3,4,5$, so $6$.</details></div>
<div class="warn"><h4>Common traps</h4><ul>
<li>Writing $|x|\gt3$ as $-3\gt x\gt3$ (impossible). "Greater than" gives two separate pieces joined by <b>or</b>.</li>
<li>Forgetting to flip when the coefficient of $x$ is negative: $|5-2x|\lt3$ gives $-3\lt5-2x\lt3$, then $-8\lt-2x\lt-2$, then $1\lt x\lt4$.</li>
<li>Treating $|u|\lt-2$ as if it had solutions.</li>
</ul></div>

Q: Solve $|5-2x|\lt3$.
L: 1
A: (1,\,4)
W: (-4,\,-1)
W: (-\infty,1)\cup(4,\infty)
W: (-1,\,4)
W: (1,\,8)
