=== LESSONS ===
<script type="text/plain" id="L-conic-para">
<h2>Parabolas</h2>
<div class="box"><h4>Focus and directrix</h4><ul>
<li>A parabola is the set of points equidistant from a <b>focus</b> and a <b>directrix</b>.</li>
<li>$x^2=4py$: vertex $(0,0)$, focus $(0,p)$, directrix $y=-p$.</li>
<li>$y^2=4px$: focus $(p,0)$, directrix $x=-p$.</li>
<li>$y=ax^2$ means $x^2=\frac1ay$, so $p=\frac1{4a}$.</li>
<li>Latus rectum (the chord through the focus parallel to the directrix) has length $4|p|$.</li>
</ul></div>
<h3>Useful moves</h3>
<ul><li>Complete the square to find the vertex, then shift the focus and directrix along with it.</li>
<li>The distance from a point on $x^2=4py$ to the focus equals its distance to the directrix: $y+p$. This turns distance problems into one subtraction.</li></ul>
</script>
<script type="text/plain" id="L-conic-ell">
<h2>Ellipses &amp; Hyperbolas</h2>
<div class="box"><h4>Ellipse $\frac{x^2}{a^2}+\frac{y^2}{b^2}=1$ with $a>b$</h4><ul>
<li>Foci at $(\pm c,0)$ with $c^2=a^2-b^2$.</li>
<li>For every point on it, the sum of the distances to the foci is $2a$ (the major axis).</li>
<li>Area $\pi ab$; eccentricity $e=\frac ca\lt 1$.</li>
</ul></div>
<div class="box"><h4>Hyperbola $\frac{x^2}{a^2}-\frac{y^2}{b^2}=1$</h4><ul>
<li>Foci at $(\pm c,0)$ with $c^2=a^2+b^2$.</li>
<li>The difference of the distances to the foci is $2a$.</li>
<li>Asymptotes $y=\pm\frac bax$.</li>
</ul></div>
<p>To read off $a$ and $b$, first divide the equation so its right side is $1$: $4x^2+9y^2=36$ becomes $\frac{x^2}9+\frac{y^2}4=1$.</p>
</script>
<script type="text/plain" id="L-conic-param">
<h2>Parametric Equations &amp; Vectors</h2>
<div class="box"><h4>Parametric curves</h4><ul>
<li><b>Eliminate the parameter:</b> solve one equation for $t$ and substitute, or use an identity ($\cos^2t+\sin^2t=1$, $\cos2t=2\cos^2t-1$).</li>
<li>$x=a\cos t,\ y=b\sin t$ traces the ellipse $\frac{x^2}{a^2}+\frac{y^2}{b^2}=1$; with $a=b$ it is a circle.</li>
<li>$x=t^2,\ y=2t$ traces the parabola $y^2=4x$.</li>
<li>Watch the range: $x=\cos t$ only gives $-1\le x\le1$.</li>
</ul></div>
<div class="box"><h4>Vectors</h4><ul>
<li>Dot product $\mathbf u\cdot\mathbf v=u_1v_1+u_2v_2=|\mathbf u||\mathbf v|\cos\theta$. It is $0$ exactly when the vectors are perpendicular.</li>
<li>Parallelogram spanned by $(a,b)$ and $(c,d)$: area $|ad-bc|$; the triangle is half that.</li>
<li>Projection of $\mathbf u$ onto $\mathbf v$ has length $\frac{|\mathbf u\cdot\mathbf v|}{|\mathbf v|}$.</li>
</ul></div>
</script>
